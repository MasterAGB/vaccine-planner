Your family’s vaccinations – in one place

Vaccine Planner (“Vakcināciju kalendārs un plāns”) helps you keep your whole family’s vaccinations and planned dates in one place, with information about the vaccines in the Latvian childhood vaccination calendar.

Coming soon to Google Play

Android app · no account · no ads · in Latvian

What the app does

  • Family profilesAdd each family member with a name and date of birth.
  • Childhood vaccination calendarInformation about the vaccines in the Latvian childhood vaccination calendar (birth to age 14). Always check current dates in the official SPKC calendar or with your doctor.
  • Completed and planned vaccinationsMark the date of a vaccination and note a planned date.
  • Vaccine informationVaccine descriptions and advice, frequently asked questions and news.
  • BackupExport your data to a file and import it on another phone. Importing replaces the data already in the app.
  • Your data stays with youNo account, ads or tracking – what you enter is stored on your phone (and in your own Android backup, if it is turned on). The developer never receives it.

Important: This app does not replace a consultation with a doctor. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

Ask your family doctor about vaccinations. Official Latvian vaccination calendar: spkc.gov.lv.
